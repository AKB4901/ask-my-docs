# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, accrued
monthly. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next calendar year;
anything beyond 5 is forfeited on December 31. PTO requests should be submitted
at least two weeks in advance for planned time off and are approved by your
direct manager.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year.
Sick leave does not carry over and does not require advance notice, but you
should notify your manager as early as possible on the day.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 6 weeks. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the
birth or adoption and can be split into a maximum of two separate blocks.

## Public holidays

The company observes 11 public holidays per year. When a public holiday falls on
a weekend, the following Monday is observed as the holiday.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be granted at the company's discretion for
personal circumstances. Requests require director-level approval and should be
made at least one month in advance where possible.
