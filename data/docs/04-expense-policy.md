# Expense and Travel Policy (FIN-118)

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses through the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase.
Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Expenses submitted more than 60
days after purchase may be declined. Reimbursement is paid in the next payroll
cycle after approval.

## Travel booking

Book flights and hotels through the approved travel platform. Economy class is
standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is permitted for flights
over 6 hours. Hotel spending should stay within the per-city nightly caps listed
in the travel portal.

## Meals and daily allowance

While traveling, the daily meal allowance is $60 in most locations and $80 in
high-cost cities such as London, New York, and Tokyo. Alcohol is not
reimbursable. Client entertainment meals require pre-approval from a director and
must list all attendees.

## Home office

Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for equipment.
An annual $200 allowance covers ongoing supplies. Furniture over $150 requires
manager approval before purchase.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times per year.
Cardholders must reconcile transactions monthly. Personal use of the corporate
card is prohibited and repeated misuse results in card revocation.
