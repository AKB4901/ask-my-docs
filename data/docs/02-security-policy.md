# Information Security Policy (SEC-2024)

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and vendors.

## Passwords and authentication

All accounts must use multi-factor authentication (MFA). Passwords must be at
least 14 characters. Hardware security keys are required for anyone with
production access. Shared accounts are prohibited; every action must be
attributable to an individual.

## Data classification

Data is classified into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Restricted.
Restricted data includes customer PII, payment details, and secrets such as API
keys. Restricted data must never be copied to personal devices or pasted into
external tools, including third-party AI assistants.

## Device security

Company laptops enforce full-disk encryption and auto-lock after five minutes of
inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@northwind.com
within one hour of discovery so the device can be remotely wiped.

## Incident reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported immediately to the security team.
Do not attempt to investigate or remediate on your own. The on-call security
engineer acknowledges reports within 15 minutes during business hours and within
one hour outside them. A post-incident review is completed within five business
days for every confirmed incident.

## Third-party software

New software or SaaS tools must be approved through the security review process
before use with any Internal or Restricted data. Vendors handling Restricted
data must have a signed data processing agreement on file.
